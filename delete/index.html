Delete your Orma account

Orma — Your Pocket Brain App, by ChaiStack

In the app

  1. Open Orma and tap You.
  2. Scroll to the end and tap Delete account.
  3. Tick Also delete my Google Drive backups if you want those gone too.
  4. Tap Delete. You need to be online. The app starts over afterwards.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, your profile, your usage counts and quota records on our side — at once.
  • Every memory, recording, picture, setting and saved AI key on the phone.
  • Your Orma backups in Google Drive, if you ticked the box. Otherwise they stay in your Drive; you can delete them any time in Google Drive (web) → Settings → Manage apps → Orma → Delete hidden app data.

Summaries of public links and recipe facts already in the shared kitchen hold nothing that identifies you and stay. Our server logs hold no names or emails and expire on their own.

No longer have the app?

Email nidhishpp.pp@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, asking us to delete your account. We delete the data above within 30 days and reply when it's done. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept on the phone.
