Orma — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-09

Orma is a personal “second brain”: you save links, screenshots, notes, voice notes and files, and the app summarises them so you can find them later by asking in plain words. ChaiStack (“we”) makes Orma. This page says what leaves your phone, where it goes, and what is kept.

What stays on your phone

  • Your memories (notes, links, summaries, tags, the search index) are kept in a database on the phone that is encrypted (SQLCipher, with a random key protected by the Android Keystore).
  • Voice recordings, pictures, covers and files are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, but these files are not encrypted.
  • Search runs on the phone with on-device models. Searching never sends your memories anywhere.
  • AI keys you paste are stored encrypted (Android Keystore) and leave the phone only to the provider they belong to.

Orma AI (the default)

To summarise a save, Orma sends what is needed to understand it to Orma AI, our service on Google Cloud Run, which asks Google Gemini on Vertex AI (region asia-south1, Mumbai). What is sent: the text of the save (a note, a page's text, text read from a picture), up to 4 small pictures, or a link. The same service answers questions in Ask (your question and the 4 best-matching saves), translates a memory when you tap Translate, and writes out voice notes (the recording, compressed). Voice notes are always written out by Orma AI, also when you use your own key.

Never stored: Orma AI keeps no copy of what you send or of the reply. Our logs hold only the endpoint, status, time taken, token counts and a hashed account id — never text, links, audio or pictures. Google does not use Vertex AI requests to train its models. The one exception is the community kitchen below.

Free accounts get a number of saves a week; Orma AI counts them against your account.

Community kitchen (you can turn it off)

When you save a public web link with Orma AI and “Help the community kitchen” is on (You → Privacy; on by default, and a card on Home asks you once), Orma AI keeps the summary it made for that link for up to 180 days, so the next person who saves the same link gets it instantly. If the link is a recipe, its facts (name, ingredients, steps, times) can join a shared recipe catalog once 3 different people have saved it. To count savers we keep only a one-way code per saver, never your account, email or notes. Private notes, pictures, voice notes and non-public links are never part of it. Turn the switch off and nothing more is added from your saves.

Your own AI key (optional)

With “Use my AI key” on, summaries and Ask answers go directly from your phone to the provider you pick (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter) with your key; that provider's privacy policy applies. We never see your key or that traffic. “Sign in with OpenRouter” uses OpenRouter's own sign-in page; the key it returns stays on your phone.

Your account

Orma needs a Google sign-in. It creates a Firebase account (Google Cloud) and a small profile: your email, Google name, Google photo link, when you first and last signed in, your app language, your plan, your choices for the community kitchen and “Orma news by email” (off unless you turn it on), and “Tell me when Pro is ready” if you tap it. Next to it we keep usage counts (how many saves, answers, translations and transcriptions per week or month, the category of a save, whether a shared result was reused) to run the weekly limit and plan the product. No memory content is in your account.

Backup (optional)

Cloud backup goes to the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, which only Orma can open; we cannot access it. The backup (your memories, recordings, pictures and settings; not AI models, not your AI key) is encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM) with a backup key that is yours. The key is saved in Google's Block Store (end-to-end encrypted) so a new phone can restore, and you can write down a 24-word recovery code. Anyone with that code can read your backups, so keep it private. You can also save a backup to a file you choose.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no third-party analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no tracking. (ML Kit's usage reporting to Google is switched off.)
  • We don't sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers above (Google Cloud, Firebase, Vertex AI) to run Orma.
  • We never receive your AI key or your backups.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you record a voice note or speak a question.
  • Notifications — progress while a save is summarised or backed up, and the optional daily “Remember this?”.
  • Biometric — optional app lock; handled by Android, the app never sees your fingerprint.
  • Internet / network state — for the uses above.

Your rights and control (India DPDP Act 2023)

  • Signing in is your consent to the processing above, for these purposes only. Marketing email is a separate choice, off by default, and you can withdraw either at any time.
  • Delete any memory in the app; it is removed from the phone. Uninstalling deletes everything on the phone.
  • Export your memories: You → Privacy → Export my data.
  • Delete Drive backups: Google Drive (web) → Settings → Manage apps → Orma → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete your account: in the app, You → Delete account (it can also delete your Drive backups); this deletes your sign-in account, your profile, its usage counts and quota records at once, and everything on the phone. Without the app, email us from your account's address (contact below) and we delete them within 30 days. Step by step: How to delete your account. Summaries of public links and recipe facts already in the shared kitchen hold nothing that identifies you and stay.
  • You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected, or name someone to act for you. Complaints go to us first (contact below), then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Orma is not directed at children under 18 and does not knowingly process their data.
