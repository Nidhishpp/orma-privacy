Orma — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Orma is a personal “second brain”: you save links, screenshots, notes, voice notes and files, and the app summarises them so you can find them later. It is built to work on your phone. We (ChaiStack, the developer) never receive your saved content.

What stays on your phone

Everything you save — notes, links, pictures, voice recordings, files, the summaries, tags and search index the app makes from them — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The on-device AI (summaries, search, voice-to-text, answers in Ask) runs on your phone and does not send your content anywhere.

App settings and any AI key you enter stay on your phone too. Your profile name and photo stay on the phone unless you use Google sign-in (see below). AI keys are stored encrypted (Android Keystore).

When the app uses the internet

Only for the things below. Online mode (You → Privacy, on by default) controls all of them except AI model downloads and Google sign-in, which happen only when you tap them. With Online mode off, the app reads no links, calls no AI provider and makes no backups.

Reading a link you savedThe website of that link (plus YouTube/Vimeo/TikTok's public “oEmbed” service for their videos)The link address, like a normal browser visit. No account data.

“Use my AI key” (off by default)The provider you pick: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouterThe text of the item being summarised or the question you ask, with the most relevant saved items. Sent directly from your phone with your key; that provider's privacy policy applies.
Google sign-in (optional, for your profile)Google, and our Firebase project (Google Cloud)Standard Google sign-in. It creates an Orma account and stores a small profile: your email, Google name, Google photo link, and when you first and last signed in. Nothing you save is included.
Cloud backup (optional)Your own Google Drive, hidden app folderAn archive of your saved items and settings (not AI models, not your AI key). Only this app can see that folder. We cannot access it.
“Buy me a coffee” (optional)Google PlayHandled entirely by Google Play’s payment screen; Google’s privacy policy applies. We never see your payment details. The app keeps only how many coffees you bought, on the phone.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking. (Google's ML Kit, which reads text in pictures on the phone, normally sends Google usage statistics; that reporting is switched off in Orma.)
  • An account only if you choose Google sign-in, holding just the profile above. We don't sell or share personal data.
  • We never receive your saved content, AI key, or backups.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you record a voice note or speak a question.
  • Notifications — to show progress while an item is being summarised and, if Resurfacing is on (You → Resurfacing), one reminder a day of something you saved a while ago.
  • Biometric — optional app lock; handled by Android, the app never sees your fingerprint.
  • Internet / network state — for the uses in the table above.

Your control

  • Delete any item in the app; it is removed from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Delete Drive backups in Google Drive (web) → Settings → Manage apps → Orma → Delete hidden app data. The app keeps at most the 3 newest backups.
  • Turn off Online mode, “Use my AI key” and backup at any time.
  • To delete your Orma account profile (from Google sign-in), email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Children

Orma is not directed at children under 13.
