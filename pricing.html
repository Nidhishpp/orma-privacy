How billing works

  • Prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes.
  • Monthly plans renew every month and yearly plans every year, until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time. You keep the paid features until the end of the period you paid for.
  • If you buy in the Android app, Google Play handles the payment. If you buy on this website, the payment is processed by our payment gateway partner. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Refunds follow our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
