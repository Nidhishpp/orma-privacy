Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Orma sells digital subscriptions only (see Pricing). Nothing is shipped, so there are no delivery or return charges.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time.
  • Bought on this website: email us from the address you used to buy, or use the cancel option in your account when it is available.
  • Bought in the Android app: open Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Orma → Cancel.
  • After you cancel, the plan does not renew. You keep the paid features until the end of the period you already paid for. Then your account moves to the Free plan. Your saved items are not deleted.

Refunds for website purchases

  • First purchase: if you are not happy, ask within 7 days of your first payment for a plan and we refund it in full.
  • Charged twice or charged wrongly: we refund the extra or wrong amount in full, whenever you tell us.
  • Forgot to cancel a renewal: ask within 48 hours of the renewal charge and we refund it, if you have not used the paid features since the renewal.
  • We remove a paid feature or stop the service: we refund the unused part of your current period.
  • Other renewals and partly used periods are not refunded. Cancelling stops the next charge.

How refunds are paid

  • Email nidhishpp.pp@gmail.com with the email address you bought with and the payment date or transaction ID.
  • We reply within 3 working days.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

Purchases through Google Play

Subscriptions and tips bought in the Android app are paid through Google Play. Refunds for them follow Google Play's refund policy. You can ask Google Play for a refund in your Google Play order history. You can also email us and we will help.

Tips

“Buy me a coffee” tips are voluntary and unlock nothing, so they are normally not refunded. If you tipped by mistake, email us within 48 hours.
